Netlify Drop has a refreshed look, with even more support for your questions and guidance on what’s next after you drop your latest site or app. We first launched Netlify Drop 13 years ago when we introduced the web to a simpler way to deploy your web project and get a custom link for it. Since that time we’ve learned a lot about how people (and now agents) use Drop.
