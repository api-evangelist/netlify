Invitations to join an existing team now show up within your Netlify dashboard, where you can accept or decline them directly without checking your email inbox. Previously, the invitation email was the only way in. If it got buried, filtered, or sent to an address you no longer check, then getting collaboration started with your teammate took a lot longer than it needed to.
