You can now pay for Netlify with Apple Pay or Google Pay, in addition to paying by credit card. Both options show up as you’d expect at checkout, so you can confirm a payment with the device you already have in hand instead of tracking down a card. It’s a small change, but it removes a genuine bit of friction—especially when you’re upgrading in the middle of shipping something and don’t want to break flow to go find your wallet.
