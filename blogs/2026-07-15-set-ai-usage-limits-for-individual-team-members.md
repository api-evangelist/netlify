Team Owners on Pro plans can now limit Agent Runners spend for individual members in your team, giving you finer control over how AI credits are used across your team. Set a single credit limit that applies to every member, then customize it with overrides for specific people who need more (or less) room to work. This makes it easy to give power users a higher ceiling while keeping predictable, budget-friendly defaults for everyone else.
