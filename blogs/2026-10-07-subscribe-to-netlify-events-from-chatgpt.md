You can now subscribe to Netlify events from ChatGPT and set up automations that respond to what’s happening on your sites. Instead of checking the Netlify dashboard or waiting on an email, you can have ChatGPT act as soon as something happens. Events you can subscribe to include: A deploy succeeded A build failed A form submission came in For more events to subscribe to, check out our deploy events.
